Where people actually stay

Phnom Penh

BKK1

The usual landing neighbourhood. Cafés, clinics, and coworking sit within a few blocks, and one-bedroom rents cluster around the mid-six hundreds when the building has a generator.

1-bed about $650/mo · walkability 82

Phnom Penh

Toul Tom Poung

A step down in rent from BKK1 with most daily needs still on foot. Power cuts are less predictable on the side streets; ask about the generator before you sign.

1-bed about $520/mo · walkability 74

Phnom Penh

Daun Penh

The riverfront and the administrative core. Useful if you want to be near the palace-side clinics and visa offices, noisier in the evening along the quay.

1-bed about $580/mo · walkability 78

Phnom Penh

Chroy Changvar

Larger apartments and more reliable building power, at the cost of a bridge crossing for almost everything. Treat walkability scores here as building-to-building, not neighbourhood-wide.

1-bed about $700/mo · walkability 46
